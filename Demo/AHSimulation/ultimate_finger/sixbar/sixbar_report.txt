== Six-bar PIP study for the 3-phalanx finger (planar, mm, finger design frame: y distal, z dorsal)
drive (fixed): stock Link-base angle psi(f), f = -90..+90 -> psi -26.7..81.5 deg; ground pivots O_M (0, 0), O_L (0, -6); P1 47.0, P2 26.0, P3 to tip site 13.85 mm
rules: one assembly branch over the whole travel; MCP, PIP monotonic straight..full; MCP(+90) in [87, 91]; hyperextension >= -35; straight pose at f >= -56 (psi_s >= -14.25, same abduction room as the current design); same-part pins >= 6.0 mm apart; new pivots |z| <= 8.0 mm in their phalanx; bars clear the PIP pin (and P1's new pin) by >= 0.5 mm in-plane
transmission: every four-bar (or equivalent four-bar) angle >= mu in the flexion range and >= min(mu, 25) over the whole travel; recommended designs use mu = 40 / 25 like synth_linkage.py
search: 116 DE runs (popsize 20, 700 generations, seeds [1, 2, 3, 4]) + bounded Nelder-Mead polish, run in parallel (16 workers); designs rounded to 0.05 mm / 0.05 deg

-- topology: with a binary ground (only O_M and O_L may be used) and the Link as the only input, the 1-DOF six-bars that
   drive both P1 and P2 are: Watt (a) [R pinned to P1 and Link, C to P2], Stephenson (b) [Link ternary: K to P1, D to P2],
   Stephenson (c) [P2 hung from the Link on two binaries X, Y], and a Stephenson with a floating ternary between Link and P2
   tied to P1 (not studied: it is (c) with one hanger moved onto P1). Every option adds 2 parts and 3 pin joints.
